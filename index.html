Never too long between meals

  • Mini sandwiches (Ivan's favourite)

  • Half an oat bar

Shop sweets and crisps

For the children

  • At most 2 packaged snacks a day, ideally about 100 kcal each (NHS).
  • Added sugar a day: ages 4–6 19 g (about 5 cubes), 7–10 24 g, 11+ 30 g (NHS).
  • Salt a day: ages 4–6 3 g, 7–10 5 g, 11+ 6 g (NHS).
  • Sweet foods with meals, not between them, are better for teeth.
  • Avoid pairing a sweet drink with a sweet snack.

In practice

  • Don't ban treats, and don't use them as rewards or punishments.
  • Serve a portion in a bowl with or after a meal, not the pack.
  • Friday film night: one small shop treat each plus popcorn.
  • If asked for sweets on other days: fruit, a yogurt lolly or popcorn, and “sweets are for Friday”.

For you

  • No routine confectionery, with small conscious exceptions. Friday is your relax night.
  • Choose one thing you really want, such as Cheez-It, Candy Kittens or a Kinder Bueno.
  • Put a portion in a bowl, sit down and enjoy it slowly.
  • Don't compensate: no fasting or “making up” the next day.
  • After long, tiring or emotional days, eat your planned snack early and have a filling dinner.

Vitamin D

The NHS says everyone should consider a daily 10 microgram supplement in autumn and winter, and children aged 1–4 all year (NHS). Under-5s should have vitamins A, C and D daily. Ask a pharmacist for the right children's product.

Children: “taste explorers”

Each dinner has one optional “explorer bite” (a pepper strip, a piece of beetroot, a kimbap roll), rated 1–5 stars on a sheet on the fridge. Praise trying, not finishing, and never pressure. They help roll sushi on Saturday, build pizzas on Sunday and fill the lolly mould.

Prep and storage

Keep it safe and simple

Leftovers

Fridge within 1–2 hours, eat within 48 hours or freeze (GOV.UK). Borscht from Wednesday is eaten Wednesday and Thursday; freeze spare portions Thursday. Lasagne is assembled Thursday and baked Friday.

Rice

Stir-fry, sushi and plov rice: cool within about 1 hour, eat within 24 hours, reheat only once until steaming hot.

Thawing

Thaw in the fridge, cook within 24 hours of fully thawing. Mince: Wednesday night. Saturday's chicken: Friday night. Sunday's chicken: Saturday night. Hake: Sunday night.

Tonight

Freeze the chicken for Saturday and Sunday as soon as you get home, plus the 150 g of thighs already at home for Sunday's plov.

Packed lunches

Use a cool bag or an ice pack.
